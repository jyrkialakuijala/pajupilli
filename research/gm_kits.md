# The drum kits against the SoundFonts, stroke by stroke

tools/gmcompare.py kits. Each General MIDI drum key at velocities 40, 64, 96, 127 on the Standard, Jazz and Brush kits (the drum channel's program 1, 33, 41), on pajupilli and on FluidR3, TimGM6mb, Jnsgm2, SGM. Measure: loudest 400 ms, K-weighted (BS.1770), each synth's stroke against its own piano (C3, C4, C5 at velocity 96 in the same file). The numbers are pajupilli less the SoundFonts' average, dB: positive = pajupilli's piece is louder against the piano than the fonts'. The Brush kit's toms, hi-hat and cymbals are compared with the Standard kit's average less 8 dB (the fonts' Brush kits play the stick's samples there). (range): how far the fonts lie apart at velocity 96.
